# SolPay Capstone Project: Strategic Foundation Document

## Part A: Final Project Proposal

### 1. Initial Project Overview

SolPay is a streamlined Solana-based payment system that enables instant crypto transactions through QR code scanning, similar to UPI but built on blockchain infrastructure. The platform focuses on SOL and USDC payments with real-time transaction verification, targeting the growing Web3 payment market with a simplified, mobile-first approach designed for rapid merchant and consumer adoption.

### 2. Core Value Proposition & Product-Market Fit

#### Value Proposition

SolPay bridges the gap between traditional payment UX and Web3 infrastructure by offering UPI-like simplicity for crypto payments. Users can generate payment QR codes instantly, accept multiple cryptocurrencies (SOL/USDC), and verify transactions in real-time without complex wallet management or technical knowledge.

#### Key Value Areas

1. **Simplicity**: Eliminates crypto payment complexity through familiar QR code interface
2. **Speed**: Sub-second transaction finality compared to traditional payment rails  
3. **Cost Efficiency**: ~$0.00025 transaction fees vs 2-3% traditional payment processing fees

#### Product-Market Fit Analysis

> The Web3 payment market is projected to reach $300.7 billion by 2032 with 28.5% CAGR, while QR code payments specifically will hit $61.73 billion by 2033 at 20% CAGR.

### 3. Key Target Markets
